Control your Dru gas fireplace from Homey. Ignite it with a safe slide-and-hold on the dashboard widget, turn it off with one tap, and set the flame height, second burner and Eco wave from the widget, the device page or your own Flows. Homey also shows the room temperature, pilot flame and fault codes.

The app finds the Dru gateway (the box the Dru app connects to) on your network automatically. It is tested with a Dru Maestro and is not made by or affiliated with Dru.
